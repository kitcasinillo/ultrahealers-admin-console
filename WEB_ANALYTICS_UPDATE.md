# Platform Analytics & Email Settings Module Update

Dear Client,

We are pleased to share an update on the newly integrated **Web Analytics & Email Notification Settings** within your Admin Console. Designed specifically for UltraHealers, these modules give your team complete, real-time visibility into website performance, user growth, and automated email messaging—all built in-house to guarantee maximum privacy, reliability, and ease of management.

---

## 🌟 Key Features & Capabilities

### 1. Unified Web Analytics & Multi-Platform Insights
* **Cross-Portal Coverage**: Track visitor activity across all three UltraHealers entry points (`ultrahealers.com`, `healers.ultrahealers.com`, and `seekers.ultrahealers.com`) in one unified dashboard.
* **100% In-House & Privacy-First**: Fully independent of third-party tracking services, ensuring complete ownership and security of user data.
* **User Growth & Acquisition Breakdowns**: Easily monitor registration trends split by **Healers** vs. **Seekers** over customizable timeframes (Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, YTD, or Custom date ranges).
* **Traffic Sources & Campaigns**: Discover exactly where your visitors are coming from—whether through search engines, referral links on partner websites, or promotional campaigns.
* **User Experience & Performance Monitoring**: Real-time tracking of page load speeds, system responsiveness, and user friction spots (such as rage clicks).
* **Instant Multi-Format Exports**: Download comprehensive reports directly into **PDF**, **Excel**, or **CSV** formats for board meetings and stakeholder presentations.

---

### 2. Email Notifications & Configuration (`Settings → Email`)

To complement user growth tracking, the **Settings → Email** panel provides full control over transactional and automated admin notifications:

* **Instant Admin Signup Alerts**:
  * Easily configure single or multiple admin recipient email addresses.
  * Receive instant email alerts whenever a new Healer or Seeker completes registration.
* **Custom Welcome Email Templates**:
  * Tailor separate welcome messages and subjects for newly registered **Seekers** and **Healers**.
  * Use smart dynamic placeholders like `{{name}}`, `{{email}}`, and `{{dashboardUrl}}` that automatically populate with real user details upon signup.
* **Live Email Preview Tool**:
  * View an interactive, real-time preview of customized welcome emails before saving or sending.
* **Built-in Delivery Testing**:
  * Test email deliverability with a single click by sending sample Seeker, Healer, or Admin notifications directly to any email inbox.
* **Secure Outbound Mail Infrastructure**:
  * Pre-configured with active TLS encryption to ensure reliable inbox delivery and prevent spam classification.
